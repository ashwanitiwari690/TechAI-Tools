Cookie Policy

This is a starting-point cookie policy for a frontend-only demonstration project. Before launch, this page should be reviewed against the specific privacy laws (such as GDPR/ePrivacy in the EU/UK, or CCPA/CPRA in California) that apply to your actual audience.

What we use instead of tracking cookies today

This site currently stores a small amount of data directly in your browser's local storage rather than traditional server-set cookies:

Category Purpose Can be disabled?
Necessary Theme preference, cookie consent choice No — required for basic site function
Preferences Saved favorites (AI tools, software, tutorials, guides) Yes — clearing your browser storage removes them
Analytics Not currently active — reserved for future use Yes, via the cookie preferences panel
Advertising Not currently active — reserved for future Google AdSense integration Yes, via the cookie preferences panel

Managing your preferences

You can change your cookie preferences via the "Manage preferences" link in the cookie banner shown on your first visit. If you've already made a choice and want to revisit it, use the button below to reset your choice — the consent banner will reappear so you can choose again.
