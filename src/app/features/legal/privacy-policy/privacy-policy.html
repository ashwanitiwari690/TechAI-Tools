What data this site currently stores

This application does not have a backend and does not operate user accounts. The following pieces of data are stored only in your own browser's local storage, and are never transmitted to us or any third party by this app:

  • Your light/dark/system theme preference
  • Your saved favorites (AI tools, software, tutorials, guides)
  • Your cookie consent choice and preferences

Third-party services

This site currently loads Google Fonts for typography. Once Google AdSense or analytics tools are added in the future (see the "Advertising" section below), those services will set their own cookies and collect data according to their own privacy policies, and this page will need to be updated to disclose that in detail.

Advertising

This site is designed to be monetized with Google AdSense in the future. Advertising placeholder components are visible throughout the site, but no real advertising code has been added yet. Once AdSense (or another ad network) is integrated, this policy must be updated to describe the data that network collects, including any use of cookies for personalized advertising, and to provide any required consent and opt-out mechanisms.

Cookie consent
